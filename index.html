Terms & Conditions * Scroll to read all ↕

Please INFORM us

  • Once your pet gets adopted (through any source), kindly update us mandatorily. Until then, our team will continuously work to get them adopted, which can take months.
  • If you are no longer looking to get your pet adopted.
  • If your pet is listed on any other adoption platforms.
  • Whether you prefer to handle the adoption yourself or opt for our team’s assistance in filtering and verifying leads so your pet goes to a safe, loving home.
  • If you handle it yourself, HappyPaws cannot take responsibility for any breeders, students, or fraudsters approaching you.
  • If you opt for our assistance, kindly respect our team’s experience and abide by our verification processes.

Please NOTE

  • We do not take any responsibility (financial or otherwise) for your pet until adoption is completed.
  • Please honestly disclose all past and current health conditions as well as any missed vaccinations.
  • Please make a well-thought-out decision before handing over your pet for adoption or trial (if successful, the pet continues in the same home and adoption formalities are finalized).
  • Dropping out of an ongoing trial or completed handover will not be possible.
  • We will archive/close the appeal if you remain unresponsive to calls/messages regarding leads or status for more than 3 follow-ups.
  • Foreign breed dogs/cats can be listed at a nominal charge of ₹100/pet, which goes directly toward sponsoring medical, nutrition, transport, and foster care until adoption.
