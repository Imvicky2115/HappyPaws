List Your Pet for Adoption

Please read the terms and conditions carefully before sharing the details to list your pet with us for adoption.

NOTE: We do not take rescue requests.

Please INFORM us:

  • Once your pet gets adopted (through any source) kindly update us mandatorily. Until then our team will be continuously working to get them adopted which can take months.
  • If you are not looking to get your pet adopted anymore.
  • If your pet is listed on any other adoption platforms.
  • If you like to handle the adoption yourself or would like our team’s assistance in filtering and verifying the leads, and facilitating the adoption so that your pet goes to a safe and loving home.
  • In case you choose to handle it yourself, we cannot take responsibility of any breeder or student or fraudsters approaching you and taking possession of your pet.
  • If you choose to opt for our assistance, kindly be respectful of the experience we hold and abide by our processes.

Please NOTE:

  • We do not take any responsibility (financial or otherwise) of your pet until he/she gets adopted.
  • Please honestly disclose about any past and current health status and misses in vaccinations, etc.
  • Please take a well thought out decision before your pet is handed over for adoption or trial (if successful, the pet continues at the same home and adoption formalities are completed to finalize the adoption).
  • Dropping out of a trial or completed handover will not be possible.
  • We shall be archiving/closing the adoption appeal in case you are unresponsive to our text/call regarding status or leads, or do not have clear responses for more than 3 iterations.
  • You can list your foreign breed dog/cat at a nominal charge of ₹100/pet. This amount goes into sponsoring medical/nutrition/transportation/foster care of our pets until adoption.
